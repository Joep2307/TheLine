The concept

Not faster.
Better.

Urban Trails Amsterdam creates alternative cycling routes through the quieter, more surprising parts of the city. Instead of the fastest route, we guide you through the most interesting one.

The routes are marked by colorful lines sprayed directly onto the ground — a playful wayfinding system that feels like discovering a secret the city painted for you. Each line leads somewhere worth finding.

How it works

Follow the line.

01

Spot the spray

Colorful lines appear on Amsterdam's streets each season, painted by local artists and communities. Each color is a different route.

02

Follow your curiosity

No app needed. Just follow the line and see where it takes you. Past canals, through courtyards, along routes you've never taken before.

03

Discover & return

Routes change every 2–3 months. New colors, new paths, new seasons. The city never gets boring when there's always something new to find.

Meet our mascot

Tod the Fox

Tod is curious, a little mischievous, and always convinced the best thing is just around the next corner. He represents everything Urban Trails stands for — the joy of wandering without a destination.

Through posters, murals, and social media, Tod appears along the routes and on city walls, encouraging people to take a different road and see Amsterdam from a new angle.

Always changing

New season,
new trail.

Routes last 2–3 months, then the city gets repainted. New colors, new neighborhoods, new artists.

Winter · Now

Blue Route

Through Oud-Zuid and the museum quarter, past frozen canals and cozy brown cafés.

Spring · March

Coral Route

Jordaan and the Negen Straatjes — spring markets, tulip stalls, freshly opened terraces.

Summer · June

Lime Route

East Harbour to NDSM Wharf — open air, graffiti walls, waterfront energy.

Autumn · Sept

Amber Route

Noord and the IJ ferry — golden leaves, quiet streets, the city at its most atmospheric.
